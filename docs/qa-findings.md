# QA findings

A browser pass over the staff app, the parent and member portals and the account flows, made in Chrome against
the local backend (fresh database, local test data only). Each finding was reproduced in the browser before it
was fixed; the fix names its pull request and how it was checked again.

Severity: **P0** data loss, security or a major business function down · **P1** a primary workflow broken ·
**P2** confusing behaviour, inconsistency, missing feedback or a recoverable defect · **P3** cosmetic or minor.

## Product decisions from this pass

- A person is on a service itself or on its stages, never both (backend #319, frontend #271).
- Servants see and change only the services and stages they are assigned to; roles are church-wide or limited to assigned groups (backend #318, frontend #269, prototype ui #9).
- A self-service erasure locks the account at once and erases it after 14 days unless cancelled; copies go out as one-time links that work once for 7 days (backend #316, frontend #262).
